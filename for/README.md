# Front pages for specific people

The playbook is general. When you send it to someone, give them a short page here that says who it's for, what they told you they want, and where to start. Keep it under a screen.

A good front page has:

- Two or three sentences in your own voice: why you're sending it, what it'll get them, "text me if you get stuck"
- What they asked you, in their words if you have them
- Their path: which guide step and which track to start on, and what to skip
- Their first real project, sized so they can finish it in a week

Keep private details out. This repo is public: no phone numbers, emails, addresses or anything they told you in confidence.

To add one, copy [ryan.md](ryan.md), rename it, and link it from the main [README](../README.md).
