# Level 2: hand off a weekly chore

The biggest wins are boring: the email you write every Monday, the notes you clean up after every meeting, the schedule you rebuild every week.

## What the AI does

The repeat work. Drafting, summarizing, reformatting, turning a messy list into a clean one.

## What you keep doing

- **You decide what good looks like.** Before handing it off, write down in one or two sentences what a perfect version has in it. That's your spec.
- **You read before anything goes out.** Every time, at least for the first month.
- **You keep the prompt.** When an ask works, save the exact words in a note (or in the Project's instructions) so next week is one paste.

## The saved-prompt pattern

```text
Every [day], I'll paste you [the raw stuff]. Turn it into [the finished thing].
Good looks like: [your one or two sentences]. Never: [the mistake you're
worried about]. Ask me if anything is missing instead of guessing.
```

When it gets something wrong, don't just fix the output. Add a line to the saved prompt so it can't make that mistake again.

## Checkpoint

One chore done by Claude three weeks in a row, from the same saved prompt, with you only reading and lightly editing.
