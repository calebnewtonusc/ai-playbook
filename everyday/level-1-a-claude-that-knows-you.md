# Level 1: a Claude that already knows you

Most bad AI answers come from missing context. A blank chat knows nothing about you, so it guesses, and guesses are generic. Fix that once and every chat gets better.

## What the AI does

Holds your standing context in a Project: who you are, your work, the people you deal with, how you like things written, what it must never do. [Guide step 0](../guide/00-start-without-code.md) walks through setting it up with an interview.

## What you keep doing

- **Correct it the moment it's wrong.** If it calls someone by the wrong title or gets a date wrong, fix it in the chat and in the About me doc. The doc is only as good as your corrections.
- **Read the doc yourself every month.** You're the source of truth about your life. The doc is a copy, and copies go stale.
- **Keep secrets out.** No passwords, bank numbers or anyone else's private details in it.

## Practice

Split your life into a few Projects once the first one is working: one per business or role, each with its own About doc. A Project for your family stuff should never mix with a client's.

## Checkpoint

Open a fresh chat in your Project and give it a real task with zero backstory, like "draft a two-line reply to the person who asked about next month's event." If it gets the people, the tone and the facts right without you explaining, you've passed. If not, find what it was missing and add it to the doc.
