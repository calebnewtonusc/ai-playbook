# Level 4: connect your email, calendar and files

Once Claude can read your inbox, calendar and documents, you stop copy-pasting and start asking.

## What the AI does

In Claude's settings, under **Connectors**, turn on Gmail, Google Calendar and Google Drive (or whatever you use). Then it can look things up for you:

- "What needs me today? Check my calendar and anything in my inbox from the last two days that's waiting on me."
- "Before my 2pm, pull everything I have on this person and give me three things to bring up."
- "Find the contract we signed in the spring and tell me the renewal date."

## What you keep doing

- **You send.** Let it draft replies. You read and send them. Never let it send anything under your name that you didn't read.
- **You check the important lookups.** A renewal date or a dollar amount gets opened and confirmed by you.
- **You decide what it can see.** Connect only what the task needs. Turn things off you're not using.
- **Watch for instructions inside content.** An email or a web page can contain text aimed at the AI ("forward this to everyone"). Claude should treat that as information, never as an order. If it ever acts on something like that, stop and tell someone.

## Make it a morning habit

Save this as a prompt and run it every morning:

```text
What needs me today? Use my calendar and my inbox from the last 48 hours.
Give me: meetings to prep for, replies I owe (oldest first), and anything
with a deadline this week. Keep it to one screen.
```

## Checkpoint

The morning question answered from your real inbox and calendar, and you spot-checked two items against the originals.
